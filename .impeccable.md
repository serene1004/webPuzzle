## Design Context

### Users
People who choose a favorite photo and enjoy a puzzle on their own during a short break. They select an image and difficulty, then complete the puzzle in the browser.

### Brand Personality
A calm, immersive, warm, and thoughtful experience. Personal photos are processed only in the browser.

### Aesthetic Direction
A spacious, dark wood puzzle table that fills the screen. Use a deep warm-charcoal page, warm off-white text, and restrained aged-brass accents. Keep the table surface simple and the gameplay controls minimal and floating so photo pieces stay in focus.

### Design Principles
- Use a full-screen canvas where pieces can be freely moved and assembled around a subtle wood-framed target.
- Keep the flow from image upload to puzzle completion short and easy to understand.
- Use familiar rounded tabs and sockets, without previewing piece locations.
- Make interaction states clear for drag, keyboard, and touch input.
- Restore the current puzzle after refresh without sending user images to a server.
